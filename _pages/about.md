I am a University of Pretoria Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Physics, Faculty of Natural and Agricultural Sciences, at the University of Pretoria, South Africa. Prior to joining UP, I held a short-term postdoctoral position in the Faculty of Electronic Engineering & Technology at Universiti Malaysia Perlis, Malaysia. I was also a visiting researcher in the Department of Quantum Mechanics at the Institute of Physics, Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń, Poland, where I worked with Prof. Karolina Słowik in the Quantum Nano-optics group.

I obtained my PhD in Theoretical Physics under the supervision of Prof. Norshamsuri Ali at the Faculty of Intelligent Computing, Universiti Malaysia Perlis. During my doctoral studies, I spent part of my PhD research as a visiting PhD student in Prof. Karolina Słowik's group in the Department of Quantum Mechanics at Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń. My doctoral research focused on thermodynamic processes and quantum correlations in hybrid quantum systems.

Before my PhD, I completed an MSc in Physics in the Theoretical Physics Group at the University of Port Harcourt, Nigeria, under the supervision of Prof. A. N. Ikot. My master's research focused on solving the Schrödinger equation for quantum systems in the presence of Aharonov-Bohm and magnetic fields using the Asymptotic Iteration Method (AIM). In parallel, I contributed to the development of the Nikiforov-Uvarov Functional Analysis (NUFA) method, a mathematical framework for obtaining analytical solutions to quantum-mechanical problems.

My current research is in the emerging field of quantum science and technology, with a particular interest in developing efficient quantum thermal devices. My research focuses on quantum thermodynamics, open quantum systems, and exactly and quasi-exactly solvable quantum systems. More recently, I have expanded my research into quantum biology, where I investigate quantum thermal machines powered by bio-inspired quantum systems.

My publications are available on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kYkyPz4AAAAJ&hl=en). I am open to research collaborations in quantum thermodynamics, quantum science and technology, open quantum systems, quantum biology, and related areas.
