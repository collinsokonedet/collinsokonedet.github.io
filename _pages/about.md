I am a theoretical quantum physicist whose research focuses on quantum thermodynamics, open quantum systems, and exactly and quasi-exactly solvable quantum systems. My work explores thermodynamic processes, quantum correlations, and the dynamics of quantum systems. More recently, I have expanded my research interests into quantum biology, where I investigate quantum thermal machines powered by bio-inspired quantum systems.

I am currently a University of Pretoria Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Physics at the University of Pretoria, South Africa.

Previously, I held a short-term postdoctoral position in the Faculty of Electronic and Engineering Technology at Universiti Malaysia Perlis, Malaysia. I was also a visiting researcher in the Quantum Mechanics Department at the Institute of Physics, Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń, Poland, where I worked with Prof. Karolina Słowik.

I obtained my PhD in Theoretical Physics under the supervision of Prof. Norshamsuri Ali at the Faculty of Intelligent Computing, Universiti Malaysia Perlis. During my doctoral studies, I spent part of my PhD research as a visiting PhD student in Prof. Karolina Słowik's group in the Department of Quantum Mechanics at Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń, Poland. My doctoral research focused on thermodynamic processes and quantum correlations in hybrid quantum systems.

Before my PhD, I completed an MSc in Physics in the Theoretical Physics Group at the University of Port Harcourt, Nigeria, under the supervision of Prof. A. N. Ikot. My master's research focused on solving the Schrödinger equation for quantum systems in the presence of Aharonov-Bohm and magnetic fields using the Asymptotic Iteration Method (AIM). In parallel, I contributed to the development of the Nikiforov-Uvarov Functional Analysis (NUFA) method, a mathematical framework for obtaining analytical solutions to quantum-mechanical problems.

My broader research interests lie at the intersection of quantum mechanics, statistical mechanics, and thermodynamics, with particular emphasis on quantum thermal machines, quantum correlations, open quantum dynamics, exactly and quasi-exactly solvable models, and emerging applications of quantum theory in biological systems.
